Participation & Payment

NMJL
All National Mah Jongg League rules apply.
Four games
Every match has four games, with each player East once.
Social vs. Focused
All matches use the same rules. Social has a more relaxed pace expectation. Focused requires a faster pace.
Proposer equipment
The current match proposer provides the Mahjong tiles, mat, racks and dice. If the proposer leaves an open match, proposer responsibility transfers to the next player who joined.
Charleston passing
All four players must place their tiles on the mat before anyone picks up incoming tiles.
Phones
No phone above table height. No photos of tiles, racks, hands or the table. No AI or electronic assistance.
Notifications
All phone notification sounds must be off.
Scoring
Winner receives NMJL hand value. Self-picked last tile +10. Jokerless Mahjong +20. Wall Game = +10 to each player and no other points for that game.
Proposer
The proposer at the time the match is completed receives +5 points.
Withdrawal
Leaving less than 24 hours before the match: −20 if no replacement joins and the match is cancelled.
Late
More than 20 minutes late: match cancelled; late player −20, other three +25 each.
Emergency
If an emergency occurs after play begins, every unfinished game becomes a Wall Game, +10 to each player.
