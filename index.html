Dragon Scramble Rules

NMJL
All American Mah Jongg League (NMJL) rules apply.
Four games
Every match has four games, with each player East once.
Charleston passing
During all Charleston passes, all four players must have placed their tiles on the mat before any player picks up the tiles being passed to them. No player may pick up incoming tiles early.
Phones
No phone above table height during play. No photos of tiles, racks, hands or the table. No AI or electronic assistance.
Notifications
All phone notification sounds must be off for the entire match.
Scoring
Winner receives NMJL hand value. Self-picked last tile +10. Jokerless Mahjong +20. Wall Game = +10 to each player and no other points for that game.
Proposer
+5 points once for every completed four-game match proposed.
Withdrawal
Leaving less than 24 hours before the match: −20 if no replacement joins and the match is cancelled.
Late
More than 20 minutes late: match cancelled; late player −20, other three +25 each.
Emergency
If an emergency occurs after play begins, every unfinished game becomes a Wall Game, +10 to each player.
