Dragon Scramble Rules

NMJL
All National Mah Jongg League rules apply.
Match format
Each match consists of four games, with each player serving as East once.
Social vs. Focused
All matches follow the same Dragon Scramble rules. Social matches have a more relaxed expectation for speed of play. Focused matches require players to maintain a faster pace.
Proposer equipment
The match proposer provides the Mahjong tiles, mat, racks and dice.
Charleston passing
During all Charleston passes, all four players must place their tiles on the mat before any player picks up the incoming tiles.
Phones
No phone above table height during play. No photos of tiles, racks, hands or the table. No AI or electronic assistance.
Notifications
All phone notification sounds must be off for the entire match.
Scoring
Winner receives the NMJL hand value. Self-picked last tile +10. Jokerless Mahjong +20. Wall Game = +10 to each player and no other points for that game.
Proposer
+5 points once for every completed four-game match proposed.
Withdrawal
Leaving less than 24 hours before the match: −20 if no replacement joins and the match is cancelled.
Late
More than 20 minutes late: match cancelled; late player −20, other three +25 each.
Emergency
If an emergency occurs after play begins, every unfinished game becomes a Wall Game, +10 to each player.
